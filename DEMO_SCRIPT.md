# MedGuard AI • Evaluator Demonstration Script & Walkthrough

This demonstration script provides an end-to-end evaluation guide for verifying MedGuard AI's document parsing, deterministic clinical rule engine, longitudinal biomarker tracking, and conversational AI.

---

## Prerequisites & Launch

1. **Start Backend Server:**
   ```bash
   cd backend
   uvicorn app.main:app --host 0.0.0.0 --port 8000
   ```
2. **Start Frontend Client:**
   ```bash
   cd frontend
   npm run dev
   ```
3. Open browser at: **`http://localhost:3000`**

---

## Step 2: Safety Alerts & Drug Conflict Inspection

1. Click **"Safety Alerts"** in the sidebar (`/alerts`).
2. Review the automated findings generated by the clinical rule engine:
   - **Severe Drug Interaction:** `Ciprofloxacin + Warfarin`
     - *Explanation:* Ciprofloxacin inhibits CYP1A2 and increases plasma concentrations of Warfarin, causing severe elevated bleeding risk and INR spikes.
     - *Recommendation:* Avoid co-administration or closely monitor INR levels daily.
   - **Duplicate Therapy / Same-Class Duplicate:** `Metformin + Glucophage`
     - *Explanation:* Brand and generic co-prescriptions of biguanide antidiabetic therapy.
   - **Allergy Contraindication:** `Amoxicillin` with Penicillin Allergy
     - *Explanation:* Patient has documented severe penicillin allergy; amoxicillin is a beta-lactam penicillin-class antibiotic with high anaphylaxis risk.
3. Test the **Risk Filter Pills** (`All Risks`, `High Risk`, `Medium Risk`, `Low Risk`) and verify smooth client-side filtering.
4. Click **"Print Report"** to verify printable clinical summary formatting.

## Step 3: Medical Timeline & Encounter Trajectory
